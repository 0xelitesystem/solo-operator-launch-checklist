# Solo Operator Launch Checklist

A practical pre-launch checklist for a solo operator shipping a product or site: the legal, security, technical, content, and distribution items to confirm before you go live. Built so one person without a team can ship something complete rather than something that looks done but is missing the parts no one sees until they break.

## The core idea

A solo operator launch checklist is the list of things to confirm before going live, grouped so nothing essential is forgotten: the legal pages, the security basics, the technical foundations, the content and conversion path, and the distribution plan. Solo operators ship alone, so there is no team to catch the missing piece. The checklist is the substitute for that second set of eyes.

## What is inside

- `01-before-you-launch.md` the mindset and the order of operations.
- `02-legal-and-policies.md` the pages and disclosures you need.
- `03-security-and-technical.md` the foundations that prevent disasters.
- `04-content-and-conversion.md` the path from visitor to action.
- `05-the-launch-checklist.md` the full checklist in one place.

## The stance

This checklist favors shipping something complete and honest over shipping something flashy and fragile. The unglamorous items, backups, legal pages, security basics, are exactly the ones a solo operator is tempted to skip and exactly the ones that cause real pain when skipped.
